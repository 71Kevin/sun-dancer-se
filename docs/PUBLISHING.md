# Distribution and permissions

Version 1.0 is published on [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/200125) and in the
[GitHub releases](https://github.com/71Kevin/sun-dancer-se/releases/tag/v1.0), with the same six packages: three
complete main files (2K, 4K and 8K; choose one) and three optional PBR add-ons for PG Patcher. The Nexus Mods page is
flagged as adult content, since the outfit carries the UBE body.

## What the packages contain

| Content | Origin | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Meshes, UVs, weights, SMP system files, textures (vanilla and PBR sets) and the plugin | Original | Free to publish |
| The UBE body in the Wrap, the UBE feet in the Sandals, the UBE arms of the first-person model, their BodySlide sliders and the simplified body copies the cloth collides with | UBE 2.0 by johnybungalow1 and lowresOutis | The same UBE parts every UBE outfit conversion carries; UBE's permissions leave conversions free ("contact me on Discord … to discuss permissions EXCEPT body/face presets, and conversions"). Credited; no UBE texture is included |
| Sliders of third-party add-ons to the UBE slider sets | Not ours | Not included (`build.ps1 -Public`, checked by `tools/dev/verify_packages.py`) |
| The enchantment's five palette textures | Recoloured from the base game's effect gradients | Base game assets modified for the same game; credited to Bethesda Game Studios |
| Screenshots | In-game captures by Tinesh | Free to publish |

Rules followed: the [Nexus Mods file submission guidelines](https://help.nexusmods.com/article/28-file-submission-guidelines)
(other authors' content only with their permission, credits, adult content flagged).
