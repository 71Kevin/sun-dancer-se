# Changelog

## 1.0 — 2026-11-22

First release, on [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/200125) and GitHub: 2K, 4K and 8K
packages and optional PBR add-ons for PG Patcher.

- Eleven light armor pieces made for UBE 2.0: Wrap, Loincloth with Briefs, Loincloth without Briefs and Briefs
  (slot 52, one replaces the other), Sandals, Bracelets, Armlets, Arm Veils, Collar, Circlet and Navel Charm.
- SMP cloth physics (FSMP) for the loincloth panels and the arm veils.
- BodySlide group "Sun Dancer" with UBE's own body, hands and feet sliders and Build Morphs support.
- Eclipse Scythe, two-handed, with the Eclipse Brand enchantment (Solar Flare, Corona and Umbra).
- Forge recipes without perks and tempering recipes; ESL-flagged plugin that only adds new records.
